Noise Tuning Cheat Sheet
========================

This cheat sheet summarizes the most important tuning parameters for the ESP32 Noise Generator. Use this when adjusting sound character, loudness, smoothness, or LED behavior.

Master Gain
-----------
gainMinDb: quietest level, recommended -60 to -40
gainMaxDb: loudest level, recommended -6 to -3
Impact: controls overall loudness and headroom

Gain Slew Rate
--------------
slewRate: 0.001 to 0.003 recommended
Impact: smoothness of volume changes, lower = smoother

Pink Noise
----------
Coefficients: a1, a2, a3
pinkGain: normalization
Impact: coloration of pink noise, deeper or brighter tone

Brown Noise
-----------
brownLeak: 0.9985 to 0.9997 recommended
Impact: rumble depth, drift speed, low-frequency weight

Blue Noise
----------
blueScale: 0.4 to 0.6 recommended
Impact: brightness and harshness of blue noise

LED Volume Bar
--------------
ledMinBrightness: 10 to 30
ledMaxBrightness: 200 to 255
ledGamma: 2.0 to 2.4
Impact: visual clarity and smoothness

Mode Flash
----------
modeFlashMs: 100 to 150 ms
Impact: responsiveness of mode change feedback

Mute Behavior
-------------
muteBrightness: 20 to 60
Impact: visibility of mute state

Network Timing
--------------
heartbeatMs: 60000 to 120000
wifiRetryMs: 10000 to 20000
mqttRetryMs: 3000 to 6000
Impact: responsiveness vs network load

Audio Buffer
------------
bufferSamples: 256 to 512
Impact: stability vs latency

Recommended Defaults
--------------------
gainMinDb: -60
gainMaxDb: -3
slewRate: 0.002
brownLeak: 0.9995
blueScale: 0.5
modeFlashMs: 120
ledGamma: 2.2
heartbeatMs: 90000
wifiRetryMs: 15000
mqttRetryMs: 5000
